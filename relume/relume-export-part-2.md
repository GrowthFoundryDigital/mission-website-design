### Section 5: FAQ Section • List • Left • Vertical
- Sitemap section name: Cleanup questions we get asked
- Tagline: Before you call
- Heading (H2): Cleanup questions we get asked
- Body: The same five questions come up in almost every cleanup conversation.
- FAQ items (each has an accordion icon, keyboard_arrow_down):
  1. How far behind is too far behind? There is no threshold we turn away. We have cleaned up files that were six months behind and files that had no reliable entries for three years. What changes is the scope, which is why we look at the file before quoting.
  2. How long does a cleanup take? One year of backlog in a single-entity business is usually a matter of weeks. Multi-year, multi-account files take longer. You get a timeline with the scope, before any work starts.
  3. What does it cost? A fixed project fee based on the scope we quote after assessing the file, not an open hourly meter. If something unexpected turns up midway, we tell you before doing the work.
  4. Will you tell us how the books got into this state? Yes, and it matters. A cleanup that doesn't address the cause repeats itself eighteen months later. We show you where the process broke down and what to change.
  5. What happens after the cleanup? Most clients move to monthly bookkeeping so it doesn't happen again. If you would rather keep it in-house or with your current bookkeeper, that works too — a clean file and a documented process help either way.
