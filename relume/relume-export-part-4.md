### Not reached or unreadable
- Link and button targets [NOT REACHED]: no link or button in the wireframe has an href.
- Hidden sub-elements [NOT REACHED]: no page or section is hidden. Many components have optional slots toggled off in Layers, such as extra taglines, button groups, logo lists and figure prefixes or suffixes. Relume doesn't render those, so any default text inside them couldn't be read. The footer's Social Link List, Image List and Company Name Image are hidden the same way.
- Sitemap descriptions: section cards have names only.

### What was captured, and where
- Hidden-state text was captured and labelled: navbar Services dropdown items, form success and error messages, the second set of carousel arrows.
- Accordions: every FAQ answer was already present in the page.
- SEO title, description and slug come from each page's Page panel.
- Component names come from the right panel when each section is selected. This project uses Relume's descriptive names rather than numbered names.

### Things that look odd
- Bookkeeping & Accounting sits at top level (/bookkeeping-accounting), not under Services, yet the navbar dropdown and footer Services column both list it with the Services sub-pages.
- Products naming: the nav label is "Products"; the footer Company column says "QuickBooks Products".
- Repeated copy: Home and About use the same three testimonials and the same three team bios. The About and Bookkeeping & Accounting CTAs are identical.
- Section shapes: the QuickBooks Cleanup process has only three steps; the others have four. The Fractional CFO, Custom Reporting and Point-of-Sale Support heroes have no icon list items; the other heroes have three.
- Placeholder assets: every image placeholder has empty alt text. The credibility logos are logoipsum-1/2/3.svg placeholders. The wireframe navbar and footer logo is the generic company-logo.svg; the MISSION wordmark appears only in Design mode.
- Footer copyright reads "MISSION Accounting. All rights reserved." with no © or year.
- Comparison sections: the wireframe has each value before its label. That order was kept and shown as value / label.

### Labels added during extraction
- Step numbers ("01", "02"…) are listed as part of each step.
- Stat values, testimonial names and companies, team roles and comparison values come from Relume's rich-text display fields, with the text unchanged.
